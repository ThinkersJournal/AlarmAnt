`docs/SYNAPSE-CHANNEL.md` (PM tasks 2026-11-19). Part A, agent to human: mail to the role `alarmant` from a role holding a send grant raises the existing escalating alert through a no-socket bridge on the Synapse machine; the acknowledgement returns as mail through a new read-only `GET /alert/:id` (open/acked only); `[PAGE v1]` format, allowlist, per-sender and overall limits, no severity levels, no quiet hours, audit without text. Part B, human unanswered to agent: an `agent` chain step (off by default) at 3N on the single setting N, routed by API-key name to a role in server config (no sender choice), a human Acknowledge cancels it, an agent reply never acknowledges or silences the alarm, loop-guard rules (skipped-self, reserved internal and bridge keys), untrusted alert text, a `GET /agent/due` board polled by the bridge, and what happens until wake-on-any-mail lands. Both directions: published `synapsed-client` (rc.123 published, rc.262 in the repo: a publish decision is open), threat models and a patent table (constraint 8 read; Part B constraints 5, 6, 9, 11, 12, 13, 15 and 16 mapped; a fresh reading, Part B first, is required before code). No code, nothing run, nothing live; AlarmAnt stores no alarms.
