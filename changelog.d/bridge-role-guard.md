kind: `alarmant-bridge` role guard
---
The bridge no longer hard-codes the Synapse role `alarmant`: it reads `ALARMANT_BRIDGE_ROLE` (default `alarmant-bridge`) and refuses to start when that is the lane role `alarmant` (also `alarmant@account`, any case), with an error that explains why. Reason: the role epoch is the role's claim counter, so any claim of `alarmant` kills the lane's own session (409 superseded); the 2026-11-24 live smoke did that. New `bridge::bridge_role` and `DEFAULT_BRIDGE_ROLE`; the binary threads the role through one `Mailbox`. `docs/SYNAPSE-ADAPTER.md` 3.2a states the rule and a reproducible smoke procedure. Verified: red-first unit tests (`the_bridge_refuses_the_lane_role`, `the_bridge_role_defaults_to_alarmant_bridge`), 229 tests, clippy `-D warnings`; no synapse command was run.
